Pricing Questions

Do I need a credit card to try AeriQuote?

No. Your trial includes 3 free property measurements & estimates and does not require a credit card.

Will I be automatically charged after my free measurements?

No. There is no automatic billing from the free trial. After your 3 free measurements, you decide whether to subscribe to Basic, Pro or Business.

What happens after my 3 free measurements?

Once the 3 free measurements are used, AeriQuote will ask you to choose a paid plan before you continue making new property measurements.

Can I create another account and get another free trial?

The free trial is intended to be limited to one trial per person or business. Duplicate or abusive trial registrations may be restricted.

What counts as a property measurement?

A measurement is counted when you use AeriQuote to perform a property measurement. Simply viewing the website or starting account setup does not use one of your free measurements.

How many measurements are included each month?

Basic includes 50 measurements per month, Pro includes 150, and Business includes 300.

Do unused monthly measurements roll over?

No, unless AeriQuote expressly states otherwise. Monthly measurement allowances reset with the applicable billing period and unused measurements have no cash value.

What happens if I reach my monthly measurement limit?

AeriQuote will show your usage and available plan options. The production account system will not silently add unapproved measurement charges.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades may take effect immediately with the applicable billing adjustment shown before confirmation. Unless otherwise disclosed, downgrades take effect with the next billing period.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Cancellation stops future renewals. Unless applicable law requires otherwise, you will generally keep access to your paid plan through the end of the billing period you already paid for.

Do you give refunds for unused time or measurements?

Paid subscription charges are generally non-refundable after a billing period begins, including unused time or measurements, except where required by law, for confirmed billing errors, or where AeriQuote approves an exception. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for full details.

Are prices in U.S. dollars?

Yes. Website plan prices are displayed in USD.

Are taxes included in the listed price?

Canadian customers may be charged applicable GST/HST and provincial taxes. U.S. customers will only be charged sales tax where AeriQuote is legally required to collect it.

Is aerial property measurement included on every plan?

Yes. Aerial property measurement is included with Basic, Pro and Business. Pro and Business also include advanced measurement tools.

Which plan includes employee accounts?

Business includes team and employee accounts as well as business dashboard and usage reporting.

Can I use my own pricing when creating estimates?

Yes. AeriQuote is designed to let the contractor use their own pricing and estimate settings.
