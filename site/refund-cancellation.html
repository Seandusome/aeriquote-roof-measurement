Refund & Cancellation Policy

Straightforward rules for AeriQuote’s free trial, subscriptions, renewals, cancellations and refunds.

Operator and email support

Monthly plans, renewal and changes

Approved monthly subscription policy — not yet implemented

Basic: USD $29/month with 25 included measurements. Pro: USD $59/month with 75. Business: USD $99/month with 200. The software tracks property lookups and new saved estimates separately, as explained above. Prices in USD. Applicable taxes are added at checkout.

Subscriptions renew monthly on the customer’s billing renewal date. The included monthly allowance resets at successful renewal. Unused monthly units do not carry forward; granted extras remain available until used.

Upgrades take effect immediately, with the price difference prorated. Usage, granted extras and the existing renewal date are preserved; the monthly allowance increases only by the difference between plans. Basic to Pro after using 23 of 25 gives 52 remaining: 2 unused plus 50 added, not a fresh 75. Downgrades take effect at the next monthly renewal.

Customers may cancel anytime to stop the next renewal and retain paid access through the current paid period. No routine prorated refunds are offered for unused time. Billing errors and exceptional cases are reviewed through email support, subject to applicable law.

Implementation status: the current allowance clock is a provisional account-anniversary period, not payment-confirmed renewal. Paid billing alignment, proration, plan-change payments and cancellation controls still need implementation and verification. The rule for failed or pending upgrade payments remains to be approved; this draft does not authorize granting unpaid benefits.

Cancellation and refund requests

Customers may cancel anytime by contacting support@aeriquote.com. The approved policy stops the next renewal and retains paid access through the current paid period. No self-service cancellation is claimed. Mailbox delivery has not yet been verified in this review.

No routine prorated refunds are provided for unused time. Billing errors and exceptional cases are reviewed through support, subject to applicable law. No blanket waiver of mandatory rights or automatic refund entitlement is implied.

Saved estimates after paid access ends

Approved post-cancellation policy — not implemented: for 120 days after paid access ends, customers will be able to view and download saved estimates in read-only mode. Creating or editing estimates will not be allowed. The exact access-expiry date must be shown. At expiry, estimates and their associated stored imagery are to be deleted from active storage, subject to reconciliation of prior commitments and required records before implementation.

The earlier approved policy describes six months of full estimate content and one year of lightweight records. It remains relevant to existing promises; the new policy will not silently shorten an existing customer’s promised retention. Grandfathering, the retention clock and treatment of lightweight records must be resolved before cleanup is enabled. No deletion job or read-only access window is enabled by this website update.

Backup expiry, legally required records and provider restrictions are separate. This policy does not promise that all backup copies disappear on day 120. Backup schedules, restore safeguards and required-record exceptions must be documented and reviewed before implementation. Imagery licensing remains unresolved; these policies do not secure imagery storage or PDF rights.

Free trial and payments

The existing offer is three free property measurements and estimates, with no card required and no automatic conversion to paid billing. Live checkout is not enabled. No payment is collected by this preview.
