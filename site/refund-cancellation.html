Refund & Cancellation Policy

Straightforward rules for AeriQuote’s free trial, subscriptions, renewals, cancellations and refunds.

Free trial promise: AeriQuote includes 3 free property measurements & estimates. No credit card is required to start, there is no automatic billing, and the free trial does not automatically convert into a paid subscription.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the rules that apply to AeriQuote free trials, paid subscriptions, renewals, cancellations, plan changes, failed payments and refund requests. It should be read together with the AeriQuote Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

2. Three Free Property Measurements

Eligible new customers may receive three free property measurements. AeriQuote does not require payment-card information to begin the free trial. When the three free measurements have been used, paid functionality may be restricted until the customer actively selects and purchases a subscription.

3. One Free Trial Per Person or Business

Unless AeriQuote expressly authorizes otherwise, the free trial is limited to one trial per person or business. Creating or attempting to create multiple accounts to obtain additional free measurements is prohibited. AeriQuote may use reasonable account, contact, device, network, business, payment and other fraud-prevention signals to identify duplicate or abusive trial registrations, subject to applicable privacy law. AeriQuote may deny, suspend or terminate trial access where it reasonably believes the trial limits are being circumvented.

4. No Automatic Conversion From Free to Paid

Using the free trial does not authorize AeriQuote to begin charging the customer. A paid subscription starts only after the customer affirmatively selects a paid plan, is shown the applicable price and billing terms, provides the required billing information and confirms the purchase.

5. Paid Subscription Plans

Paid subscriptions provide the features and monthly measurement allowance shown for the selected plan at the time of purchase. Unless a different billing period is clearly disclosed at checkout, paid plans are billed monthly in advance.

6. Automatic Renewal of Paid Subscriptions

Once a customer voluntarily purchases a paid subscription, that paid subscription will automatically renew for successive billing periods until cancelled, unless otherwise stated at checkout. The applicable subscription fee and taxes will be charged using the authorized payment method at each renewal.

7. Cancelling a Paid Subscription

Customers may cancel through the account billing controls when available or through the published AeriQuote support channel. Cancellation stops future renewal charges. Unless applicable law requires otherwise, cancellation does not retroactively cancel the current paid billing period.

8. Access After Cancellation

Unless the account is suspended for misuse, fraud, non-payment or another permitted reason, a customer who cancels will generally retain the paid features of the selected plan through the end of the already-paid billing period. At the end of that period, paid functionality may be restricted or the account may move to the access level then offered by AeriQuote.

9. General Refund Rule

Because customers can evaluate AeriQuote using three free property measurements before purchasing, subscription charges are generally non-refundable once a paid billing period has begun, except where required by law, where AeriQuote confirms a billing error, or where AeriQuote expressly approves an exception.

10. Partial Periods, Unused Time and Unused Measurements

Unless required by law or expressly stated otherwise, AeriQuote does not provide refunds or credits for partial billing periods, unused subscription time, unused measurements, unused features, voluntary account inactivity, cancellations made after a renewal charge, or a customer's decision not to use the service during an already-paid period.

11. Measurement Allowances

Each paid plan includes the measurement allowance displayed for that plan. Unless AeriQuote expressly states otherwise, unused monthly measurements do not roll over into later billing periods, are not transferable between unrelated accounts and have no cash value.

12. Upgrades

A plan upgrade may take effect immediately. Before an upgrade is confirmed, AeriQuote will disclose the applicable price or billing adjustment through the account or checkout experience. The production billing system may apply an appropriate prorated adjustment when supported by the payment provider.

13. Downgrades

Unless otherwise disclosed before confirmation, a downgrade will take effect at the start of the next billing period. The customer will normally retain the current paid plan through the end of the already-paid period. Downgrading does not create a refund for the difference between plans during the current billing period.

14. Failed, Declined or Reversed Payments

If a payment is declined, fails or is reversed, AeriQuote may retry the payment where permitted, request an updated payment method, provide notice, and restrict paid functionality if payment remains outstanding. A failed payment does not require AeriQuote to permanently delete the customer's account or business records immediately.

15. Duplicate or Incorrect Charges

Customers who believe they were charged incorrectly or more than once should contact AeriQuote promptly with the account email and relevant transaction information. Confirmed AeriQuote billing errors will be corrected, including an appropriate refund or credit where applicable.

16. Payment Disputes and Chargebacks

Customers are encouraged to contact AeriQuote first when they believe a billing error has occurred so the issue can be investigated. AeriQuote may suspend an account associated with fraudulent or abusive payment disputes or chargebacks. Nothing in this policy limits a customer's lawful rights to dispute an unauthorized or incorrect transaction with a financial institution or payment provider.

17. Taxes, Currency and Payment Processing

Plan prices are displayed in the currency identified on the pricing or checkout page. Applicable sales, use, GST/HST, provincial, state or similar taxes may be added where required. Payment-card information should be collected and tokenized by AeriQuote's payment provider rather than stored directly by AeriQuote when live payment processing is enabled.

18. Account Data After Cancellation

Cancellation of a paid subscription and deletion of an account are separate actions. AeriQuote may retain account, estimate, customer, billing and transaction information for legitimate business, security, legal and recordkeeping purposes in accordance with the Privacy Policy. Before account closure or loss of paid access, customers should export or retain business records they are legally or operationally required to keep. AeriQuote will publish any specific post-cancellation data-access or deletion period before live subscriptions are launched.

19. Account Suspension or Termination

AeriQuote may suspend or terminate access as permitted by the Terms of Use, including for material violations, fraud, abusive trial activity, unlawful use, security threats or unresolved payment problems. Refund eligibility following a suspension or termination will depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

20. Promotions and Special Offers

Promotional pricing, discounts or special subscription offers may have additional terms disclosed when the offer is presented. Unless expressly stated otherwise, promotional offers do not alter the general cancellation and refund rules in this policy.

21. Changes to Pricing or Billing Terms

AeriQuote may change plan pricing or billing terms prospectively. Where required, customers will receive advance notice before a pricing change applies to a future renewal. A customer who does not wish to continue under the new price may cancel before the applicable renewal.

22. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or waives refund, cancellation, consumer-protection or other rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable Canadian, U.S., provincial, territorial or state law.

23. Contacting AeriQuote About Billing

AeriQuote will publish its final billing-support email address and support contact details before live payment processing is enabled. Customers should use the official support channel shown on the AeriQuote website or inside their account.
