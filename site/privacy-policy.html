Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AeriQuote may collect, use, disclose and protect personal information when people visit the website, create an account or use AeriQuote’s property measurement and estimate tools.

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Important: This is a detailed working privacy draft based on AeriQuote’s current planned product. Before launch, it must be reconciled with the production system: legal entity, Privacy Officer, hosting/database locations, payment processor, map/imagery providers, analytics, cookies, email providers, retention schedule and actual security/incident procedures.

1. Scope and Accountability

This Privacy Policy describes how the organization operating AeriQuote (“AeriQuote,” “we,” “us”) handles personal information through the AeriQuote website, accounts, trials, subscriptions, property measurement tools, estimate workflows, support and related services. AeriQuote will designate a person responsible for privacy compliance and publish that person’s title and contact information before public launch.

2. Roles: AeriQuote, Business Customers and Their Customers

AeriQuote serves contractors and other businesses. In some situations AeriQuote determines why and how personal information is used, such as account administration, billing, security and direct support. In other situations, a business customer enters information about its own customers, employees or jobs and directs AeriQuote to process that information to provide the software. The business customer remains responsible for its own privacy notices, consents and lawful collection/use of information.

3. Categories of Information We May Collect

3.1 Account and identity information

Name, company name, business contact details, email address, telephone number, country, province/state, website, account identifiers, login-related information and account role.

3.2 Business configuration information

Industry, services offered, property types, pricing method and defaults, taxes, estimate defaults, branding, logos, customer-facing messages, warranties or service notes and other setup preferences.

3.3 Customer and job information entered by business users

Customer names, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses, job notes, property addresses, estimate records, quoted prices, taxes, payment/status fields, service selections and other records a business user chooses to store.

3.4 Property, imagery and measurement information

Property searches, street addresses, geographic coordinates, map/aerial imagery references, drawn points or polygons, roof/service-area measurements, pitch or surface information, added structures, property images and resulting measurement outputs.

3.5 Subscription and transaction information

Plan, billing cycle, subscription status, payment status, invoices or transaction identifiers, applicable taxes and related records. Full payment-card details may be collected and processed directly by a payment provider rather than AeriQuote.

3.6 Device, log and usage information

IP address, browser/device type, operating system, timestamps, login events, feature activity, pages/screens viewed, measurement and estimate usage, error logs, security events and diagnostic information.

3.7 Support and communications

Messages, support requests, feedback and information supplied when communicating with AeriQuote.

3.8 Cookies and similar technologies

Session, authentication, security, preference and—if enabled—analytics or marketing identifiers. A final cookie inventory will be completed before launch so this Policy and any cookie notice accurately reflect deployed technologies.

4. Sources of Information

We may receive information directly from account holders and users; from employees or administrators of a business account; from customers when a business user enters their job information; automatically from browsers/devices; from payment and support providers; and from mapping, geocoding, aerial-imagery or property-data providers used to fulfill property searches and measurement workflows.

5. Purposes for Collection, Use and Disclosure

We may process information to:

  • create, authenticate, administer and secure accounts;
  • configure AeriQuote based on industry, services, pricing, taxes, branding and estimate preferences;
  • search for properties, display maps or imagery and generate measurements;
  • create, save, retrieve and generate customer estimates and PDFs;
  • track measurement allowances, plan usage and account activity;
  • process subscriptions, billing, taxes and account changes;
  • provide support, respond to inquiries and troubleshoot problems;
  • detect abuse, fraud, unauthorized access and security incidents;
  • maintain, test, analyze and improve reliability and product functionality;
  • send operational, billing, security and legal notices;
  • send marketing communications where permitted and subject to applicable consent/unsubscribe requirements;
  • establish, exercise or defend legal rights and comply with legal obligations.

6. Consent and Other Lawful Authority

Where consent is required, AeriQuote will seek consent in a form appropriate to the sensitivity of the information and the reasonable expectations of the individual. Some processing is necessary to provide a requested account or service, while other optional uses may require a separate choice. Individuals may withdraw consent to optional processing where legally permitted, subject to reasonable notice and consequences explained at the time.

7. Limiting Collection

AeriQuote intends to collect only information reasonably needed for identified business and service purposes. Product forms and workflows should avoid requesting information that is not necessary for the relevant account, measurement, estimate, billing, support or security purpose.

8. Information About a Contractor’s Customers

Business account holders may enter personal information about homeowners, property contacts, commercial clients or other customers. The business account holder is responsible for ensuring it has authority to collect and use that information and to instruct AeriQuote to process it. AeriQuote uses that information to provide the requested software functionality, maintain/security the service and otherwise as permitted by this Policy and applicable law.

9. Disclosure to Service Providers

AeriQuote may use service providers for cloud hosting, databases, mapping/geocoding, aerial imagery, payments, email or communications, analytics, security, error monitoring, file storage, customer support and related infrastructure. Providers may process information only for authorized purposes subject to appropriate contractual or other safeguards. Before launch, AeriQuote will maintain an internal provider/data-flow inventory and update public disclosures where required.

10. Other Disclosures

Information may also be disclosed: when directed or authorized by the relevant user; to comply with law, court orders or lawful government requests; to investigate fraud, security or violations; to protect rights, safety or property; to professional advisers under appropriate duties; or in connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, sale or transfer of all or part of the business, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

11. We Do Not Treat Customer Data as a Product for Sale

AeriQuote’s business model is subscription software. AeriQuote does not intend to sell contractor customer lists or customer/job records as a data-broker product. If future practices materially change, this Policy and any legally required choices or notices must be updated before the new practice begins.

12. Cookies, Analytics and Online Tracking

Essential cookies may be used for authentication, security, session continuity and preferences. If AeriQuote deploys analytics, advertising pixels or non-essential tracking, those technologies, purposes, providers, retention and user choices will be documented in the final cookie notice and consent mechanism where required. Users should not be told that AeriQuote uses a specific analytics or advertising provider until it is actually deployed.

13. Accuracy

AeriQuote seeks to keep personal information as accurate, complete and current as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is used. Account holders can help by keeping their account and business information current and by correcting customer/job information they control.

14. Safeguards

AeriQuote will use administrative, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity, amount and use of information. The production security program should address access controls, authentication, least-privilege access, secure configuration, encryption where appropriate, patching, backups, logging/monitoring, vendor management, employee confidentiality/training, incident response and secure deletion. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

15. Security Incidents and Breach Response

AeriQuote will maintain procedures to identify, investigate, contain, document and respond to suspected privacy or security incidents. Where applicable law requires notification to affected individuals, regulators or others, AeriQuote will make the required assessment and notifications. Required breach records will be maintained where applicable.

16. Retention and Deletion

Personal information will be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for identified purposes, legal/accounting requirements, dispute resolution, fraud/security needs and legitimate business records. Different categories may have different retention periods. Before launch, AeriQuote will establish a documented retention schedule covering account data, customer/job records, estimates, logs, billing records, support records and backups.

Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove information from encrypted backups or legally required records; such information will be isolated from ordinary use and expire according to applicable backup/retention processes.

17. Cross-Border Processing

Depending on the final production providers, information may be processed or stored in Canada, the United States or other jurisdictions. Information processed outside an individual’s province/state or country may be subject to the laws and lawful access regimes of that jurisdiction. AeriQuote will confirm production data locations and make any additional disclosures required before launch.

18. Access and Correction

Subject to applicable law and permitted exceptions, individuals may request information about the existence, use and disclosure of their personal information, request access, and challenge its accuracy or completeness. Identity verification may be required before fulfilling a request. If a request concerns information controlled by an AeriQuote business customer, AeriQuote may direct the individual to that business or assist the business as appropriate.

19. Withdrawal of Consent and Account Choices

Where processing depends on consent, individuals may withdraw consent where legally permitted. Some information is necessary to provide an account, subscription or requested service; withdrawing consent to essential processing may mean AeriQuote cannot continue providing that feature or account. Marketing messages will include legally required unsubscribe options.

20. Privacy Questions and Complaints

Individuals may raise questions or complaints about AeriQuote’s privacy practices. AeriQuote will investigate and respond in accordance with applicable law and its privacy-management procedures. Before launch, the name or title and contact information of the person accountable for privacy will be published here.

21. Canadian Privacy Rights and Requirements

Depending on the activity and jurisdiction, Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and/or substantially similar provincial private-sector privacy laws may apply. AeriQuote’s privacy program is intended to address principles including accountability, identifying purposes, consent, limiting collection, limiting use/disclosure/retention, accuracy, safeguards, openness, individual access and challenging compliance.

Alberta, British Columbia and Quebec have private-sector privacy legislation that may apply in place of or alongside federal requirements depending on the circumstances. AeriQuote will review jurisdiction-specific requirements as the service launches and expands.

22. United States Privacy

U.S. privacy obligations vary by state, data type, business size and activity. Before launch and as AeriQuote grows, AeriQuote will assess whether state privacy laws require additional notices or rights such as access, correction, deletion, portability, appeal or opt-out mechanisms. Where legally required, jurisdiction-specific supplements or request methods will be provided.

23. Children

AeriQuote is business software intended for contractors, property-service businesses and their authorized users. It is not directed to children and AeriQuote does not intend to knowingly solicit children to create business accounts.

24. Automated Measurements and Decisions

AeriQuote may automate calculations or measurement-related outputs, but contractors choose pricing and are responsible for reviewing estimates and making business decisions. If AeriQuote later introduces automated decision-making that creates legal or similarly significant effects for individuals, this Policy will be updated as required.

25. Business Transfers

If AeriQuote is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring or sale of assets, information may be disclosed or transferred as permitted by law and subject to appropriate confidentiality, security and notice requirements.

26. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy as AeriQuote, its providers or legal requirements change. The current version will display an effective date. Material changes will receive additional notice when required by law or appropriate in the circumstances.

27. Privacy Officer and Contact Information

Before public launch, AeriQuote will insert the full legal business name, Privacy Officer (or accountable title), mailing address and privacy email/contact method here. The same information will be made readily available wherever required for access requests and complaints.
