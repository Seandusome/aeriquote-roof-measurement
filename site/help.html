From your first property to a finished estimate.

Practical steps for measuring, pricing, customer estimates and account help.

1. Create your first estimate

Roofing: start with AutoMeasure, then review the roof and add any missing roof separately.

This changes the guide only. Your account’s Business Type controls the measuring workflow.

  1. Enter and check the property

    In Step 1, type the address, choose the matching suggestion and check the city and province. For the practice address, confirm Saskatoon and SK. Click the property-loading button, then check that the aerial shows the correct property.

    If an address cannot be matched, open Can’t locate the property? Use dropped pin / coordinates. Enter latitude first, then longitude; confirm the aerial before measuring.

  2. Measure only the work you will quote

    Roofing: review AutoMeasure’s roof coverage. Use + ADD GARAGE / OTHER ROOF for an additional roof; review pitch information and every included section before continuing.

    Surface work: choose + ADD AREA, trace the outside edge using at least three points, then click FINISH & SAVE AREA. Add another service area when you need a separate surface.

    Distance work: choose + LINEAR, click the start, turns and endpoint, then click FINISH & SAVE LINEAR. Review Measurements for This Job and the saved totals.

  3. Check this job’s price

    Review the measurement, rate, additional charges and tax settings in Step 3. Area uses square feet; Linear uses linear feet. Where you have separate service areas, review each area’s rate and the combined total. Make sure the quote summary reflects the price you intend to charge.

  4. Add customer details

    In Step 4, enter the customer’s name, phone, email and relevant notes. Use clearly labeled test details for practice, then review what appears in the customer estimate.

  5. Create, review and download

    In Step 5, review optional additional services and image choices, then click CREATE CUSTOMER ESTIMATE. Check the branding, wording, measurements, rates, taxes, total and image framing. Click DOWNLOAD ESTIMATE PDF and open the downloaded file to check it.

    Use PREVIOUS ESTIMATES to find and reopen the saved estimate. Before leaving a job, verify it appears there and keep a downloaded copy. For initial testing, attach that PDF to your own email when sending it to a customer.

Help does not measure the practice property or change your job. Review aerial measurements before quoting; confirm uncertain details on site.

2. Choose and correct a measurement tool
  • AutoMeasure: automatic roof measurement where coverage is available. Check that the intended roof sections are included.
  • Area: lawns, driveways, parking areas and other surfaces charged by square foot. Save each area before continuing.
  • Linear: gutters, fencing, curbing, edging and other lengths. Save the line before continuing.

While drawing, UNDO LAST POINT removes the latest point. Clear controls affect the current tool; read their labels before using them. For a saved area or line, use its correction/edit control and save the changes. Check the job’s measurement list afterward.

Correcting an outline on the customer estimate is a picture adjustment; it does not change measured quantities or price. To correct the measurement itself, return to the measuring view and review the total again.

3. Set pricing, taxes and company branding

Price for This Job is the amount charged per unit for the current quote. Check the displayed unit and the quote summary after changing it. Separate service-area rates can affect the combined price.

Business owners can open Dealer Profile / Branding to review company contact details, logo, default rates and tax settings. Employee accounts have limited settings access; ask the business owner to make account-level changes.

Confirm your country and province/state, taxes-included versus regular pricing, and any additional services. Review the customer-facing estimate before sending it. Avoid assuming the displayed defaults suit every job.

4. Download, check and reopen estimates

Create the customer estimate, review its images and wording, then choose DOWNLOAD ESTIMATE PDF. Open the file from your browser’s downloads and check every page before sending.

Use PREVIOUS ESTIMATES to search by customer or address. Choose OPEN to reopen an estimate or VIEW HISTORY for earlier versions.

If the PDF is not appearing, check browser download permissions and finish any active image/outline edit. If the problem continues, keep the measuring page open, note the property and contact support. If you cannot find a saved estimate, confirm you are signed into the right business.

5. Get back into your account

Choose Forgot password and enter your login email. Check the inbox and spam folder, then use the newest reset link. Links expire after 30 minutes and can be used once. Business owners and employees reset their own passwords.

If you forgot the email, use Contact & Support and provide your business name and a reachable email. Ownership must be verified before access can be recovered. Never send passwords, reset links or card details.

For a disabled employee login, contact the business owner. An employee cannot reactivate their own login.

6. Understand allowances and keep your records

Your plan includes a measurement and estimate allowance. Review View plans / Upgrade for the published plan quantities. If you see a limit message, note its exact wording and contact support; do not repeatedly reload a property to bypass it.

Download final PDFs and keep your own business copies. Six months of full estimates and one year of lightweight records is the planned retention policy; automatic expiry is not active yet. We will explain any expiry process before it is enabled.

Before closing a job, check its saved entry in Previous Estimates. If an error occurs, do not assume a save completed simply because the preview appeared.
