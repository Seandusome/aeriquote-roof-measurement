About AeriQuote

Property measurement and estimate software designed to help contractors move from address to quote faster.

Built Around the Way Contractors Actually Quote Work

AeriQuote is being built to shorten the distance between a new lead and a professional estimate. Instead of driving to every property before a contractor can even price the opportunity, AeriQuote brings property measurement, the contractor’s own pricing and estimate creation into one workflow.

Measure

Enter a property address and use available aerial/property information and measurement tools to determine the service area.

Price

Use your own pricing and business defaults. AeriQuote is not trying to tell contractors what they should charge.

Quote

Turn the measured property and selected pricing into a professional customer estimate.

Built for Multiple Industries

Roofing, snow removal, pressure washing, paving and sealcoating, landscaping and commercial property services can use the same core idea with industry-specific workflows.

Our Product Principle

Measure it. Price it. Quote it. Without driving to the property first.

AeriQuote is designed to make the first stage of estimating faster while keeping the contractor in control. The contractor defines what is being measured, what the service means, how the job is priced and what ultimately goes to the customer.

Built for Real Business Use

The product is being designed around branded estimates, customer/job information, measurement allowances, team capabilities and business usage reporting—not just a stand-alone measuring tool.

Canada & United States

AeriQuote is being developed for contractor and property-service businesses in Canada and the United States, subject to available imagery, property data and service coverage.
