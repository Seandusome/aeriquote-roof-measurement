# AeriQuote | Emergency recovery guide

FOR THE BUSINESS OWNER - October 30, 2026
Keep this guide available outside the app. You do not need to code.

## 1. Protect the customer first

If a measurement or total looks wrong, stop sending estimates and PDFs for the affected workflow. Tell the contractor not to rely on the affected quote until checked. Do not guess a corrected total or repeatedly press Save.

## 2. Keep the evidence and work

Leave the browser tab open. Do not clear browser storage, log out or refresh if work is unsaved. Write down the time, error message, industry, browser and steps taken. Take a screenshot with private customer details covered. Never send passwords, reset links or API keys.

## 3. Report and escalate

If AeriQuote opens, submit a bug report and keep its reference. In Head Office Support, mark an internal note P1 for wrong totals, lost data, security or an outage; P2 for a blocked save or PDF with work preserved. Do not open private business estimates to triage a ticket.

## 4. If the whole site is down

Check status.render.com, then contact your technical responder through a channel outside AeriQuote. Do not assume the in-app form can work during an outage. Existing support inbox: sean@aeriquote.com; verify it is monitored and reachable. It is not a substitute for a technical responder.

## 5. Let the responder recover safely

For a bad update: ask for a verified compatible application rollback while keeping the current customer database. For missing or corrupt data: preserve the current database and ask Render Support about recovery into a NEW database. An old backup does not contain newer estimates. Never delete a database, restore over live data or deploy random older versions.

## 6. Resume only after verification

The responder must check login, a disposable test estimate, saved measurements, pricing and both PDFs, then confirm the affected workflow is safe. Record the incident, working release and what remains unresolved. Ask affected contractors to recheck their quotes before sending them.

**Before launch:** Name a technical responder and backup contact, verify alert delivery and database recovery. These are not yet confirmed. Do not treat this guide as proof that production recovery is ready.
