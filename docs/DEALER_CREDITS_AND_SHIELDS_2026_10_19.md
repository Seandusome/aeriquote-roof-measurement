## Administrative role hardening
Review found that the legacy Head Office check used an editable owner name. Head Office authorization now uses a persisted owner-ID role, seeded once from existing Head Office accounts, or the existing primary office email. Later profile/signup name changes cannot grant that role. Isolated regression renames an ordinary QA owner to Head Office and verifies both admin routes still reject it. Existing seeded office roles survive company-name edits; employees remain excluded. Future additional Head Office logins need explicit protected provisioning, not a display-name change.
